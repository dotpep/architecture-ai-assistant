sequenceDiagram
    participant C as Customer

    activate O

    activate I
    I-->>O: Inventory response
    deactivate I
    O->>P: Get product details
    activate P
    P-->>O: Product details
    deactivate P
    O->>PM: Process payment
    activate PM
    PM-->>O: Payment response
    deactivate PM

    deactivate O
    GW-->>C: Order confirmation
    deactivate GW
